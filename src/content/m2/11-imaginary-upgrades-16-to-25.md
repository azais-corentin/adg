## How this list works

Every upgrade below is one-time: meet its task, pay its iM price, and its effect is permanent. Tasks check continuously, and most can be **locked** so you cannot fail them by accident — lock first, then attempt. Costs run from 1e9 up to <Num value="1.6e15" /> iM, so expect several cap-raising Reality pushes between purchases. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:159-323 -->

## Notes on the awkward ones

**15 — Fabrication of Ideals.** Enter Eternity Challenge 2 with Infinity Dimension autobuyers off. EC2 forbids Infinity Dimensions, so you cannot fail the "never owned ID1" check inside it; just push antimatter to <Num value="1e1500000000000" />. Use the upgrade lock to block buying IDs as well. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:159-177 -->

**16 — Massless Momentum.** See [Destabilizing Lai'tela's Reality](/guide/m2/destabilizing-laitelas-reality): the check only needs your highest still-enabled Dimension tier at 6 or fewer (two sub-30-second destabilizations). The first two fast runs come cheaply; later tiers need bigger multipliers. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:178-188 -->

**17 — Chiral Oscillation.** Raise your Singularity cap (the +/− controls on the Lai'tela tab) until one condense yields 20+ Singularities, then condense with auto-condense above the cap threshold. Entropic Condensing levels (upgrade 10) raise the yield per condense. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:189-199 -->

**18 — Dimensional Symmetry.** Counts Antimatter plus Replicanti plus Tachyon Galaxies together, so push all three: a normal late-game Reality with active galaxy farming gets there. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:200-211 -->

**19 — Deterministic Radiation.** A restricted Reality: buy at most 8 Time Studies, push Tickspeed Continuum to 3.85e6. Lock the upgrade first so a 9th study cannot slip in, skip the study-heavy rows, and lean on Continuum-boosting glyphs. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:212-226 -->

**20 — Vacuum Acceleration.** The Continuum bonus display on the Lai'tela tab must read +100% or more; Dark Matter growth gets you there passively. Besides the 10x iM refill, it unlocks autobuyers for upgrades 1–10, which ends the manual rebuy chore. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:227-239 -->

**21 — Existential Elimination.** Turn Continuum off on the Autobuyers page for the whole Reality (lock the upgrade so enabling it is blocked), then push to <Num value="1e7400000000000" /> AM. A normal glyph set without Continuum scaling still gets there late in the stage. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:240-255 -->

**22 — Total Termination.** Inside Effarig's Reality, equip cursed (negative-count) glyphs until 4 slots' worth of curse, then push to <Num value="1e150000000000" /> AM. Cursed Effarig glyphs from your inventory work; the last slot may hold a normal glyph. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:256-271 -->

**23 — Planar Purification.** Unequip everything in Ra's Reality and push gained glyph level to 20,000. Level comes from your glyph-level factors, not equipped glyphs, so stack level factors and Alchemy Glyph-sacrifice effects first. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:272-286 -->

**24 — Absolute Annulment.** In Ra's Reality with the Black Hole fully inverted (charged and discharged to the extreme), hold 13,000 Antimatter Galaxies at once. Build galaxies before inverting, and lock the upgrade so discharging or un-inverting is blocked mid-attempt. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:287-306 -->

**25 — Omnipresent Obliteration.** Fully destabilize Lai'tela's Reality (all 8 tiers, so the button reports no active Dimensions), then Reality from inside it with at most one non-companion glyph equipped. This buys you Pelle. Details in [Destabilizing Lai'tela's Reality](/guide/m2/destabilizing-laitelas-reality). <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js:307-323 -->

<Callout kind="warning">

Upgrades 11–14 and 20–23 stop working inside Doomed Reality (Pelle); upgrade 24 keeps working, and so do 15 (Lai'tela/Continuum) and 25 (Pelle) — the game never takes your Celestials away. Buy everything you can before Dooming. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/imaginary-upgrades.js: isDisabledInDoomed on 11–14, 20–23 only -->
