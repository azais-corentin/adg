## What Tesseracts do

Tesseracts unlock on completing the Nameless Reality. Each one permanently raises the purchase cap of **all Infinity Dimensions** — the cap that otherwise stops ID upgrades and throttles their multipliers. The count never resets, so every Tesseract keeps paying in all later Realities.

## Costs

Tesseracts are bought on the Infinity Dimensions tab with Infinity Points. Costs rise steeply (the first costs <Num value="1e20000000" /> IP — a 1 followed by 20 million zeroes — and each next one climbs far faster), but each Tesseract is much stronger than the last, widening the cap by a growing amount.

Your IP bar shows progress toward the next Tesseract as a percentage, and the Infinity button highlights when one is affordable — no need to check the tab constantly. Buy the moment one lights up; the ID cap jump outweighs anything else that IP could do.

</Callout>

## How to push

Each block of ID purchases multiplies Dimension output, so the loop after the Nameless is simple:

1. Run short amplified Realities for RM, shards and Glyphs.
2. Spend the IP on Tesseracts the moment they are affordable.
3. Higher ID caps raise every run's ceiling, which raises the next run's IP, which buys the next Tesseract.

Glyph-wise, keep three sets saved in presets: a pushing set (Power/Time with your Effarig Glyph), a shard variety set (many distinct effects), and a level set for claiming good rolls. The filter from Effarig keeps the inventory clean while you farm.

## Toward V

The Nameless Ones do not unlock the next Celestial — V unlocks through achievements (a Galaxy challenge plus simultaneous totals). The Tesseract-driven economy is what gets your RM, Eternities and Infinities to those totals; the V articles pick up the exact requirements.
