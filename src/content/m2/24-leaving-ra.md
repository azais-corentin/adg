## The exit gate

Ra's stage ends at <Num value="1e1000" /> Reality Machines: reaching the cap unlocks Imaginary Machines, the next layer. Every system in Ra feeds that number — pet-level multipliers, alchemy powers, triad studies, charged upgrades — so RM growth is the scoreboard for the whole stage. When gains stall, the fix is always one of: higher pet levels, fuller alchemy, more Theorems, or a better Glyph set.

<StageGate until="imaginary-machines" mode="highlight">

Not at the cap yet? The loop is: Ra runs for Memories → levels and unlocks → stronger pushes → more RM → longer runs → repeat, with V runs interleaved for Theorems.

</StageGate>

## Signs you are ready

- Teresa 15+ (Perk Shop caps, strong charges) and Effarig 15+ (shard-scaled Glyph levels).
- Alchemy base resources capped or near-capped, Decoherence online.
- At least the first two Triad studies bought.
- V runs steadily adding Theorems for V-memory growth.
- RM within a few orders of magnitude of <Num value="1e1000" /> and climbing each Reality.

Do not wait for level 25s across the board — the last pet levels are long grinds that Imaginary Machines speed up rather than gate.
