## Unlocking them

Challenges 10, 11 and 12 stay locked until you have completed **16 Infinities**. The count, not IP, is the gate — fast small crunches get you there sooner than slow big ones. At 16 the three cards light up in the Challenges tab alongside the first nine.

<!-- lockedAt 16 Infinities: src/lib/data/generated/challenges.json -->

## The three

- **C10 — six Dimensions only.** Only 6 Dimensions exist, and Boost/Galaxy costs change. Reward: the **Dimension Boost autobuyer**. Play it like an early-game run: Boosts matter more than ever since two Dimensions are simply gone.
- **C11 — normal matter.** A second resource rises once you own a 2nd Dimension, and if it ever exceeds your antimatter it forces a Boost (without the multiplier). Reward: the **Galaxy autobuyer**. Keep antimatter ahead: spend matter down by buying, and Boost deliberately before it overtakes you.
- **C12 — tangled Dimensions.** Each Dimension produces the one two tiers below instead of one below (1st and 2nd both make antimatter; even tiers are the workers). Reward: the **Big Crunch autobuyer** — the single most important reward in this whole stage.

<!-- descriptions: src/lib/data/generated/challenges.json (normal 10-12) -->
C12's production web is confusing on paper but plays fine in practice: buy everything, keep even tiers fed, and let the doubled-up bottom tiers carry you. All three are easier than C9 — players who cleared C9 first usually walk through these.

## Suggested order and preparation

Clear C10 and C11 before C12 if you go in numeric order — each unlocks an autobuyer (Boosts, then Galaxies) that speeds the next attempt. You want a comfortable IP cushion (all the cheap Infinity Upgrades bought) and a few dozen Infinities behind you; none of the three demands the precision C9 did. If any of them stalls, the answer is the same as everywhere in this stage: leave, crunch a few more times for Infinities and IP, and come back stronger.
<Callout kind="tip">

C12 first if you only want one: the Big Crunch autobuyer automates crunching, which compounds into faster Infinities, which feed every Infinities-scaling upgrade you own. Set it to crunch after a few seconds and let it run.

</Callout>

## After all twelve

With every Normal Challenge complete, every autobuyer is upgradeable with IP in the Autobuyers tab: lower intervals (faster firing) and bigger bulks (more per firing). The next step is pushing the Big Crunch autobuyer's interval to its floor — that is literally the key to [breaking Infinity](/guide/m1/maxing-crunch-autobuyer).
