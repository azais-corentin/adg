## Unlock it, then sacrifice everything spare

**Scour to Empower** (group 4, <Num value="1.5e3" /> RM) unlocks glyph sacrifice: removing a glyph feeds its value into a permanent per-type bonus instead of just freeing space. Its unlock needs 30 total glyphs at once, so stop deleting anything once group 4 is in sight and let the inventory fill. <!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/reality-upgrades.js -->

After that, every duplicate or weak glyph is sacrifice fuel. Keep your best worn set plus one spare set; sacrifice the rest. Teresa's Reality later multiplies all sacrifice values, so early sacrificing is never wasted.

## What each type pays

| Glyph type | Permanent bonus | Why it matters |
| ---------- | --------------- | -------------- |
| Power (Ω) | Distant Galaxy scaling starts later | Galaxies stay cheap far longer; one of the strongest bonuses |
| Infinity (∞) | Bigger multiplier when buying 8th Infinity Dimensions | Direct Infinity Dimension power |
| Time (Δ) | Bigger multiplier when buying 8th Time Dimensions | Direct Time Dimension power |
| Replication (Ξ) | Replicanti Galaxy scaling starts later | Replicanti Galaxies stay cheap far longer |
| Dilation (Ψ) | More Tachyon Particles | Faster Dilation stretch of every run |
| Effarig (Ϙ) | Extra glyph rarity | Better rolls forever (post-Effarig) |

Power and Replication sacrifice push galaxy scalings back — the same walls that gate your EP pushes — so most players feel those two first. Dilation sacrifice is quiet but compounds across every run's Tachyon gain.

## How to do it on Android

Tap the sacrifice control on the unwanted glyph and confirm. With the unlock bought, the Reality button also offers to sacrifice the newly gained glyph instead of keeping it — say yes for anything that does not beat your worn set. The Sacrifice Type panel shows each type's current bonus; the Glyph Filter panel can auto-sacrifice new glyphs below your standards when the filter unlocks later.

<Callout kind="warning">

Before Scour to Empower, deleting glyphs gives **nothing**. If the inventory is full and the upgrade is close, wear your best, protect the rest in protected rows, and wait — do not throw glyphs away for space.

</Callout>
