## Row 3: 50 RM each

Five upgrades at <Num value="50" /> RM. Two change how the game plays; three are passive income.

| Upgrade | Unlock | Effect |
| ------- | ------ | ------ |
| The Boundless Flow | 1e12 banked Infinities | Gain 10% of your per-Infinity Infinities every second |
| The Knowing Existence | <Num value="1e70" /> EP Eternity without ever completing EC1 | EP multiplier from Reality and Theorem count — do before your first EC1 clear |
| The Telemechanical Process | <Num value="1e4000" /> EP Eternity without buying Time Dimensions 5–8 | Better Eternity autobuyer plus TD and ×5 EP autobuyers |
| The Eternal Flow | 1e7 total Eternities | Eternities per second equal to your Reality count |
| The Paradoxical Forever | <Num value="1e10" /> EP without buying the ×5 EP upgrade | Tachyon gain from your ×5 EP multiplier — buy the ×5 upgrade right after |

<!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/reality-upgrades.js -->

The Knowing Existence is the trap: completing EC1 even once locks it for that run, so do this Eternity before touching EC1. The Paradoxical Forever is the opposite — deliberately hold off the ×5 EP upgrade for one Eternity, then buy it.

## Row 4: 1,500 RM each

| Upgrade | Unlock | Effect |
| ------- | ------ | ------ |
| Disparity of Rarity | Reality with 4 Uncommon-or-better glyphs equipped | Better glyph rarity rolls |
| Duplicity of Potency | Reality with 4 glyphs of 2+ effects equipped | 50% chance of an extra effect on new glyphs |
| Measure of Forever | Reality with 4 glyphs of level 10+ equipped | Eternity count raises glyph level |
| Scour to Empower | Own 30 glyphs at once | Unlock glyph sacrifice — the big one |
| Parity of Singularity | 100 days total play time after Black Hole unlock | Unlock the second Black Hole |

<!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/reality-upgrades.js -->

Scour to Empower changes the game: every spare glyph becomes permanent per-type bonuses (see Glyph sacrifice). Stop deleting glyphs once this row is in sight — you need 30 at once for its unlock. Parity of Singularity needs the Black Hole first, so days only start counting after that unlock.

## Row 5: 100,000 RM each

| Upgrade | Unlock | Effect |
| ------- | ------ | ------ |
| Cosmic Conglomerate | 2,800 total galaxies of all types | Remote Antimatter Galaxy scaling moves to 100,000 galaxies |
| Temporal Transcendence | <Num value="1e28000" /> Time Shards | Time Dimension multiplier from days in this Reality |
| Replicative Rapidity | Reality in under 15 minutes game time | Replicanti speed from your fastest Reality |
| Synthetic Symbolism | Reality for 5,000 RM with no glyphs equipped | Fifth glyph slot |
| Effortless Existence | Best <Num value="1e11111" /> EP | Reality autobuyer and Automator command |

<!-- vendor/ad-source/src/core/secret-formula/reality/reality-upgrades.js -->

Buying all five (plus everything above) completes the set of 20 one-time upgrades and earns the Master of Reality achievement — which unlocks Teresa. Synthetic Symbolism's glyphless Reality is easier than it sounds: unequip everything, run with sacrifice bonuses and upgrades only, and Reality at 5,000 RM.
